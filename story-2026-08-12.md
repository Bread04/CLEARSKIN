## Story Structure

### Opening Hook

> *"I've had eczema since I was a baby. My entire life, I've watched my skin flare without ever knowing why."*

### Core Narrative

#### Minute 1: The Scar (0:00–1:00)

Raise your hand if you or someone you know has eczema.

*(pause)*

1 in 5 Singaporeans. I'm one of them — since I was a baby.

For my entire life, I've struggled to detect what causes my skin to flare. I cut out prawns. Then eggs. Then dairy. My skin still flared. I spent over SGD 600 on allergy panels — the results said I wasn't allergic to anything. My dermatologist said "try to reduce stress."

That's not an answer.

The problem wasn't that I wasn't tracking. The problem was I was only tracking one thing at a time. My flares weren't caused by one trigger. They were caused by the combination — shellfish on a humid day, after a bad night's sleep. No app connected those dots.

So I built one.

#### Minute 2: The Problem — Sharpen the Pain (1:00–2:00)

This isn't just my problem. There are 1.1 million eczema sufferers in Singapore. Add IBS, food allergies, asthma — that's a massive chunk of this room.

The average person takes 12 to 36 months to identify their triggers. Years of flare-ups. SGD hundreds wasted on specialist visits. And the tools they're using? Western apps that don't know what Char Kway Teow is. Food diaries that don't account for Singapore's 85% humidity. Spreadsheets that can't detect patterns.

Nobody has built this for Singapore. Until now.

#### Minutes 3–4: The Solution — Live Demo (2:00–4:00)

**Conversational logging:**
*"No forms. Just tell ClearLah how you feel — like texting a friend."*

Type: "Had laksa, stressed from work, skin super itchy tonight." The AI parses Singlish, understands food slang, and auto-fills across 5 pillars: food, lifestyle, skincare, symptoms, and auto-pulled NEA weather. Then it asks: "Did I get this right?" — and learns from your corrections.

**NEA weather auto-fill:**
*"Singapore's humidity, PSI, UV — pulled automatically. You never log weather."*

**Hawker Decoder:**
*"Type any hawker dish — in English, Malay, or Chinese. See exactly what's in it, and your personal risk score."*

147 dishes. Hawker, restaurant chains, international cuisine.

**The AI insight — the "wow" moment:**
*"This is what took me a lifetime to figure out. ClearLah surfaces it in 3 weeks."*

Not just "avoid shellfish." But: shellfish + humidity above 85% + less than 6 hours sleep. Any two of these three, and you flare. That's a multi-variable correlation no human can compute in their head.

**Ask ClearLah:**
*"Can I eat laksa today?"*

The AI cross-references your personal triggers, today's live NEA weather, and the hawker database. Answer: "Today's humidity is 88% — and shellfish at high humidity is one of your strongest triggers. On Aug 7, you ate laksa and your skin hit 8/10 by evening. Maybe swap for chicken rice today, lah."

That's an AI agent reasoning about YOUR body — citing YOUR evidence. Not generic advice.

**High Risk Day alert:**
*"This morning, ClearLah sent an alert. Humidity is 88% today. Based on my profile — high risk day. I'll watch what I eat."*

**Doctor report:**
*"One tap. A structured report with top triggers, confidence levels, symptom trends, and evidence timeline. Bring it to your dermatologist. Not 'I think it might be prawns' — actual data."*

#### Minute 5: The AI Agent — The Real Product (4:00–5:00)

Here's what makes ClearLah an AI agent, not an app with AI features.

Most health apps are forms with reminders. ClearLah is a detective and an advisor — it finds what's hurting you AND tells you what to do about it.

The AI agent does four things no tracker can do:

1. **It understands you** — Singlish, food slang, incomplete sentences. No dropdowns, no forms.
2. **It reasons across 5 dimensions simultaneously** — food, sleep, stress, skincare, humidity. A human can't hold those in their head at once.
3. **It finds the WHY** — not just WHAT triggers you, but WHY the pattern exists, using evidence from your own data. It looks at your lifestyle patterns and surfaces the underlying causes.
4. **It learns** — every day you log, your personal model improves. Thumbs up or down on parse accuracy. The agent gets smarter. That's the data moat no competitor can ever copy — because they'd need to live your life to build it.

The pattern engine? Pure TypeScript. But the reasoning layer — the part that says "this combination matters, here's why, and here's what to do about it" — that's the AI agent.

Built entirely on CodeBuddy. Solo. 7 days. That's the power of coding with AI.

#### Minute 6: Business Value — The Market (5:00–6:00)

1.1 million eczema sufferers in Singapore alone. Near-identical problem across Malaysia and Indonesia — same hawker food culture, same tropical climate.

The business model:
- **Free tier** — basic logging and hawker decoder
- **Premium** — SGD 9.90/month for advanced AI insights and doctor reports
- **Clinic partnerships** — anonymised population trigger data for dermatologists
- **Insurance integrations** — preventative health data for wellness programmes

At just 1% penetration of Singapore's eczema market — 11,000 users — that's SGD 1.3M ARR on the premium tier alone.

The data moat is the real business: every user's personal trigger profile is irreplaceable and grows more valuable over time. No competitor can replicate a user's 90-day personalised trigger map.

#### Minute 7: The Vision — Dream Big (6:00–7:00)

Today, ClearLah is a trigger detection agent. But the data we're building — millions of anonymised logs connecting food, lifestyle, climate, and symptoms — that's something bigger.

Imagine a world where dermatologists prescribe ClearLah before any allergy test. Where insurance companies reward you for understanding your triggers. Where the NEA sends ClearLah users a high-risk day alert every time the PSI spikes.

We're not just building an app. We're building the first personalised health intelligence layer for Singapore's unique environment.

ClearLah was built by a Singaporean, for Singaporeans, with local data from day one. It's my story. It's 1.1 million people's story. And we built it in 7 days on CodeBuddy.

## Usage Guidelines

### Best Channels

- **Live pitch** — this is designed for the stage. Practise the first 90 seconds until automatic.
- **Demo video** — record a backup screen recording following the demo flow in Minute 3–4.
- **Submission page** — use Short Blurb + Solution sections from ClearLah-Submission.md.
- **Social media** — the Short Version above works for Twitter/LinkedIn post-pitch.
- **Networking** — one-liner: *"ClearLah is Singapore's AI health detective — it tells eczema and allergy sufferers exactly what's triggering their flares."*

### Audience Considerations

- **Judges:** Lead with the AI innovation. Make them understand the AI IS the product. Say "built entirely on CodeBuddy" at least twice.
- **Fellow hackers:** Lead with "7 days, solo, CodeBuddy." They'll respect the execution.
- **Potential users:** Lead with the personal story and the Hawker Decoder. It's the most relatable feature.
- **Investors:** Lead with the market size (1.1M), SEA expansion, and data moat.

### Tone and Voice Notes

- **Personal, not corporate.** First person. "I" not "we."
- **Vulnerable, not polished.** The scar is the strength.
- **Authoritative from experience, not credentials.** Don't sell — tell.
- **Singaporean.** Say "Singapore" at least 5 times. Use "lah" naturally, not forced.
- **Confident, not arrogant.** The product speaks. The demo is the proof.

### Adaptation Suggestions

1. **If you only get 90 seconds:** Open with the personal story, state the 1.1M number, show ONE feature (Hawker Decoder or Ask ClearLah), and close with the tagline.
2. **If the demo fails:** Have the screen recording queued. Say "let me show you what it looks like" and play the video. Never panic.
3. **If a judge asks "isn't this just a tracker?":** "Three things a tracker can't do: understand Singlish, reason across 5 dimensions simultaneously, and explain WHY a pattern exists using evidence from your own data."
4. **If asked about medical liability:** "ClearLah is a patient-reported data tool. Every report is labelled 'not a medical diagnosis.' We empower better doctor conversations — we don't replace doctors."
