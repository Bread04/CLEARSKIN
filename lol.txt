VI. FAQ
Q1: I don’t know how to code — can I still take part?
Yes! Both CodeBuddy and WorkBuddy let you generate code and applications through natural-language conversations, so no coding background is needed to enter.

Q2: Can I take part on my own?
Yes. You can enter as an individual or form a team (up to 3 members per team).

Q3: Can I submit multiple projects across the three directions?
Yes. You’re welcome to enter more than one direction, and to submit more than one project per direction.

Q4: Can I edit my projects after submitting?
Yes. You can sign in to the website and update any submitted projects at any time before the submission deadline.

Q5: Is the pitch round held online or in person?
The format will be confirmed on the event website. Once you are shortlisted in the Top 10, we will notify you in advance of the exact format, timing and logistics.

Q6: Must my projects use Tencent Cloud products?
Yes. Every project must be built on at least one of CodeBuddy or WorkBuddy, and must include proof of usage — otherwise it will not proceed to scoring.
